# Rescue and Disaster Management System

## Problem Statement

During natural or man-made disasters such as floods, earthquakes, fires,
building collapses, road accidents, and industrial accidents, people
often need immediate rescue and medical assistance. In such situations,
communication between victims, emergency authorities, rescue teams,
hospitals, police, fire departments, and other agencies can become
difficult. Delays in receiving information, locating victims, assigning
rescue teams, and providing medical assistance can result in loss of
lives and resources.

The **Rescue and Disaster Management System** is designed to provide a
centralized platform for reporting, monitoring, coordinating, and
managing emergency situations. A person facing an emergency can use the
system to report the incident by providing details such as the type of
disaster, number of people affected, severity of the situation, and
current location. The system can capture the user's location using GPS,
reducing the time required to identify the affected area.

Once an emergency is reported, the emergency dispatcher can verify the
information and assess the severity of the incident. Based on the
location, severity, available resources, and type of disaster, the
system helps the dispatcher select and assign the nearest suitable
rescue team. Rescue teams receive the incident details and can update
their status and location while travelling to the affected area.

 the system can coordinate
with nearby hospitals or medical teams and provide information about the
number of victims and their medical requirements. Police, fire
departments, ambulance services, and other emergency agencies can also
be notified when their assistance is required. This improves
coordination and avoids unnecessary delays between different emergency
response teams.

The system also supports real-time monitoring of rescue operations.
Authorities can monitor the status of an incident, the location of
assigned rescue teams, available resources, and the number of people
rescued. For large-scale or critical disasters, the system can send
emergency alerts to people in affected areas so that they can evacuate
or take necessary safety measures.

The system maintains records of incidents, rescue operations, resources
used, victims rescued, medical assistance provided, and emergency
responses. These records can be used by administrators and authorities
to generate reports and analyze the effectiveness of disaster response.

The main objective of the Rescue and Disaster Management System is to
**reduce emergency response time, improve coordination between emergency
agencies, efficiently utilize rescue resources, and increase the chances
of saving lives during disasters**.
